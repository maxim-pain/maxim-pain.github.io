02

Collaborative work — my part

what I drove

In-pipeline sensor register control

Making register writes actually reach the sensor

My contribution: diagnosed why control tags were being silently dropped inside the camera framework, fixed the data-width encoding, and built a property-based side-channel for per-camera targeted register read and write — verified across both cores and every camera.

Wireless connectivity & remote debug

Debugging both cores over the air, reliably

My contribution: drove the WiFi integration on top of a teammate’s app — concurrent client and hotspot operation, self-healing debug-over-WiFi to both cores, stable addressing via mDNS, a relay path to the second core, and channel-coexistence handling to stop high-throughput transfers from dropping.
