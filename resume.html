01

Summary

From bare metal to working product — that is the job. The domain has changed five times in twenty-five years — virtual avionics operational with air forces, defence mission computers, autonomous-driving compute under ISO 26262 / FuSA practice, regulated medical devices, industrial control, and now AR glasses — but the requirement never has: it is not allowed to fail.

Across eleven roles I have owned the layers most engineers will not touch: bootloader, BSP, kernel, drivers, RTOS, multi-SoC and vendor bring-up on hardware that has never booted — then held it to hard real-time under sustained load. My working rule throughout: solve the root cause, never patch the symptom. No workarounds, no deadlocks, no crash dumps, every frame and every deadline met, and never a reboot as a recovery strategy.

I have hired and led two teams (~10 engineers), held principal-level titles twice, acted as technical authority across domains, and owned architecture and design end to end. Because I also build the build systems, CI/CD and release pipelines around my own platforms, I can carry a product from first power-on to a signed, shipping image without handing off the hard parts.
