Platform / Systems Engineer Snke XR [20XX] — present
  • Full-stack bring-up of a dual-SoC AR glasses platform: two symmetric compute cores over a multi-gigabit PCIe link, flashed and booted as one device.

  • Root-caused cross-core DMA misrouting and PCIe link-recovery faults; built coordinated dual-core boot and deploy.
  • Built the tooling the team runs on: layered device-tree/overlay build system, containerised reproducible builds and signed artifact pipeline, a desktop DevTools app for the test fleet, and an on-device C++ camera/sensor service with SDK and viewer.
